# Content review checklist

The chapters were drafted by writers working from NCERT, and each was checked by `tools/pack-builder/dist/validate.js` for schema, coverage and real asset ids. They still need review by a subject teacher before classroom use. Below are the points each writer flagged. Kannada in every chapter needs a Kannada-medium teacher's review.

Where the correct option was moved to balance answers across A–D, numeric options can appear out of order (for example "2, 3, 4, 5" shuffled). Reorder them during review.

## 1st PUC Chemistry, ch 6–9

- **Syllabus scope:** assumes the rationalised syllabus keeps Redox 7.4 (electrode processes), Organic 8.9–8.10 (qualitative/quantitative analysis), and Hydrocarbons 9.2.4 (conformations) and 9.6 (carcinogenicity). Redox titrations are left out.
- **Values used:**
  - E° for Li −3.05, Na −2.71, Mg −2.36, Zn −0.76, Fe −0.44, Cu +0.34, Ag +0.80 V
  - ethane torsional barrier ≈ 12.5 kJ/mol
  - pentane-isomer boiling points 309 / 301 / 282.5 K
  - AgBr molar mass taken as 188 in the Carius problem
- **Questions to check:**
  - pH of 10⁻⁸ M HCl ≈ 6.98
  - oxidation state of S in H₂SO₅ is +6
  - pent-1-en-4-yne numbering
  - 4 monochloro products of 2-methylbutane, ignoring stereoisomers
  - cyclopentadienyl anion is aromatic and cycloheptatriene is not
  - the peroxide effect applies to HBr only
  - chlorobenzene is slower than benzene and ortho/para directing
  - Kjeldahl fails for nitro, azo and ring nitrogen
  - F₂ does not disproportionate
  - carbanions are sp³ and pyramidal
- **Long concepts:** the English narration reads only the first three sentences of long concept bodies (19 of 44); the full text is on screen.

## 2nd PUC Maths, ch 1–7

- **Syllabus scope:** the following are treated as deleted and left out: binary operations, properties of determinants, elementary row operations, mean value theorem, tangents/normals/approximations, and the definite integral as a limit of a sum. Inverse-trig 2.3 keeps only the basic properties; check how much the current book keeps.
- **Outside the textbook:**

  - the Ch 4 trick uses adj(adj A) = |A|ⁿ⁻²A, common in KCET/JEE
- **Notation:**
  - matrices in quiz options are written as "[a b; c d]" in plain text; check this reads well on the board
  - cot⁻¹(−√3) = 5π/6 uses NCERT's (0, π) range
- **Kannada terms to check:** ಸ್ವವಾಚಕ, ಸಂಕ್ರಾಮಕ, ಏಕೈಕ/ಮೇಲ್ಮುಖ/ದ್ವಿಮುಖ, ಮಾತೃಕೆ, ನಿರ್ಧಾರಕ, ಉಪನಿರ್ಧಾರಕ, ಸಹಗುಣಕ, ಸಹಜ (adjoint), ಸಂತತತೆ, ಅವಕಲನೀಯತೆ, ಸಮಾಕಲನ, ಪ್ರಾಚಲ.

## 2nd PUC Biology, ch 1–7

- **Syllabus scope:** linkage/recombination, polygenic inheritance, pleiotropy, the Human Genome Project, DNA fingerprinting, the lac operon and the brief account of evolution are assumed to still be in the book.
- **Ch3:** MTP figures follow NCERT's statement of the 2017 amendment (one doctor up to 12 weeks, two doctors for 12–24 weeks), but the amendment was passed in 2021; check against the current book. Population figures: about 350 million at independence, over 1.2 billion in 2011.
- **Ch2:** a question says relaxin is not a placental hormone; this relies on NCERT's line that the ovary secretes relaxin in late pregnancy.
- **Ch4:** skin colour controlled by 3 genes; Morgan's recombination figures 1.3% and 37.2%.
- **Ch5:**
  - Human Genome Project: 3164.7 million bp, about 30,000 genes, chromosome 1 has 2968 genes and Y has 231, 1.4 million SNPs.
  - E. coli replicates in about 18 minutes.
  - Ochoa is credited with polynucleotide phosphorylase.
- **Ch6:**
  - universe about 20 billion years old; life about 4 billion years ago
  - Miller's experiment at 800 °C
  - coelacanth caught in 1938
  - cranial capacities 650–800 / 900 / 1400 cc
  - modern Homo sapiens from 75,000–10,000 years ago
- **Ch7:**
  - opioid receptors in the CNS and gut
  - heroin is a depressant
  - the hepatitis B vaccine is made in yeast
  - MALT is about 50% of lymphoid tissue
  - the `virus` 3D model is SARS-CoV-2, captioned as a generic enveloped virus
- **Tricks:** the mnemonics are invented memory aids, not NCERT text.
- **Kannada:** check the transliterated terms (ಟೇಪಿಟಮ್, ಸಿನರ್ಜಿಡ್ …) and ನಿಷೇಚನ, ಭ್ರೂಣಪೋಷಕ, ಪ್ರತಿಲೇಖನ.
